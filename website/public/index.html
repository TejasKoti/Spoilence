01 / The shield

Less plot.
More play.

The everyday scroll can give away more than you asked for. Spoilence looks for potential GTA VI spoilers on supported YouTube, Instagram and TikTok layouts, then hides the item or protects its details.

Hide the whole item.

The item disappears completely. No bar, placeholder or leftover gap.

Keep the space. Blur the details.

The thumbnail is blurred and the title becomes a generic line. Time, views and channel name stay visible on supported layouts.

Try both modes. This is a visual demo, not live detection. Some spoilers can still get through.

04 / Questions

Before you
head back out.

Will it catch every spoiler?

No. Image-only spoilers, unsupported languages and changed page layouts can get through. Spoilence currently uses local text rules and accessible metadata. OCR and image classifiers are not enabled.

Does it work in Opera GX and Brave?

The Chromium bundle includes setup routes for Brave, Opera, Opera GX, Vivaldi, Chrome and Edge. These browsers share extension APIs, but individual fork testing is still pending. Installed Chromium, Edge and Firefox have prior synthetic validation. Compatible does not mean individually certified.

What about Safari or mobile?

Mac Safari 18.4 or later has a temporary folder-install route. Actual Mac Safari testing is still pending. This release is for desktop browsers. There is no mobile app, APK, IPA or executable installer.

Why does Firefox need loading again?

This is an unsigned development extension. Firefox's temporary add-on loader removes it when the browser closes. A signed release would change that installation path; one is not available in this package.

Can I switch it off and clear its data?

Yes. Open the extension's controls to switch protection off, change sensitivity, configure sites or reset local data. OFF clears decisions and restores content. After removing or externally disabling the extension, reload open tabs to remove any injected covers.
